skif is released under the MIT licence. Code derived from other projects is credited in
[`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](https://github.com/Goldziher/skif/blob/main/ATTRIBUTIONS.md).

- **[sherif](https://github.com/QuiiBz/sherif)** (MIT, Tom Lienard and contributors). skif began as a fork of
  sherif and was rebuilt as a restructured codebase. The workspace and manifest rules and their autofixes are
  ported from it.
- **[syncpack](https://github.com/JamieMason/syncpack)** (MIT, Jamie Mason and contributors). The specifier model
  is vendored in `skif-specifier`, and the version-group and semver-group policies follow syncpack's design.
- **[knip](https://github.com/webpro-nl/knip)** (ISC, Lars Kappert and contributors). The JavaScript analysis
  follows knip: its issue types, its plugin knowledge (enablers, config files, entry patterns), the plugins
  enabled by ancestor workspaces, and the list of global binaries that scripts may run without a dependency are
  taken from it and re-implemented on [oxc](https://oxc.rs).

See [`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](https://github.com/Goldziher/skif/blob/main/ATTRIBUTIONS.md) for upstream commits,
copyright notices and modifications.
